Last updated: {{ updated }}

This policy explains what information {{ entity }} ({% if entity != "BSynch" %}"BSynch", {% endif %}"we") collects when companies and their people use the BSynch platform at bsynch.com, how it is used, who it is shared with and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Each company that uses BSynch (for example, a contractor and its crew) decides what work information goes into its account. For that information we act as a service provider that processes it on the company's behalf and only under its instructions. For account information (your sign-in, security events) we are responsible. Questions: {{ contact }}.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your company's data to train AI models.

3. Cookies and storage on your device

BSynch uses one sign-in cookie to keep you signed in, plus storage in your browser for preferences such as theme and the last screen you used. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Who we share it with

Only with providers that run the service for us, under contracts that limit their use of the data:

  • Render – application hosting (United States).
  • Supabase – database (United States).
  • Anthropic – reads a photo of a receipt or ticket when you ask the app to fill in the details. Only that image is sent.
  • Email providers (Resend, Google, Namecheap Private Email) – deliver the invoices, estimates and alerts your company sends.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of the business (with notice to you).

5. Who inside BSynch can see your company's data

Your company's data is private to your company: other companies on BSynch never see it. The owner of the BSynch platform can open it to run, secure and support the service; every time that happens it is logged and visible to your company's Manager. Other platform administrators can open it only when your company's Manager grants support access for a limited time (24 or 72 hours), and every screen opened during that time is logged and visible to the Manager.

6. How long we keep it

  • Location points: {{ location_days }} days.
  • Work information, invoices and records: while your company's account is active, or as your company decides, and as long as the law requires for business and tax records.
  • Security logs: kept to protect accounts and investigate incidents.
  • When a company closes its account we delete or return its data on request, except what the law requires us to keep.

7. How we protect it

Encrypted connections (HTTPS) only; passwords and PINs stored as one-way hashes; optional or required two-step verification; role-based access; limits on repeated sign-in attempts; database access only from our servers; and a log of security-relevant actions. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and your company as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

You can see and correct your account details in the app, turn location off in your phone settings, and ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. Depending on where you live (for example Texas or California), you may have additional rights, and we will not treat you differently for using them. If the information belongs to your employer's account, we may forward your request to them. Write to {{ contact }}.

9. Children

BSynch's business tools are for adults and are not directed to children. The separate Home Lab family feature stores information about children only when a parent or guardian enters it; see the Home Lab notice.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell your company before material changes take effect.
