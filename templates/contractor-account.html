Samsung / Android: add a “Dictate” icon
  1. Install the app (button below, or Chrome menu → Install app).
  2. Press and hold the BSynch icon on the home screen.
  3. Drag Dictate work order to the home screen. One tap opens it with the microphone ready.
Bixby: say a phrase to open it
  1. Open Bixby → menu → Quick commands → +.
  2. Phrase: “nuevo trabajo”. Action: Open app → BSynch.
  3. Turn on the option above so the app opens on dictation.
iPhone
  1. In Safari, open this dictation link, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Name it “Nuevo trabajo”. You can then say “Hey Siri, open Nuevo trabajo”.

⚠️ Only dictate while parked, or let a passenger do it.

Privacy

📍 Your location is shared only while the app is open on your screen (about every 5 minutes, and when you save a visit). It stops as soon as you switch apps, lock the phone or close the app. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings.
