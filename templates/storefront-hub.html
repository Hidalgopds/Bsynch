Storefront

An always-current online catalog for local stores (appliances, furniture, mattresses, open-box), with no typing: take a photo, set the price, it's listed.

Being built · only platform admins see this

What it will do (first version)

  1. Photo in, product listed: photo of the product and its label; AI fills brand, model, size and specs.
  2. Price and quantity, then publish.
  3. Sold / On hold with one tap, so the catalog never shows what's gone.
  4. Public catalog per store, made for phones, with an Ask on WhatsApp button on each product.
  5. Share a product to Facebook, Marketplace or WhatsApp status in one tap.
  6. English and Spanish; layaway ("apartado") friendly.

Before building: check stores want it

  • Visit 5 stores: how do they sell today, who posts photos, how long it takes.
  • Would they pay (for example $29/month) for listings that make themselves?
  • If 2 or 3 say "yes, when?", the Storefront conversation builds the screens.
